# Cote Juros: Visual Assets Direction

This note keeps the credit journey visually consistent while real Freepik assets are selected.

## Direction

Use sober, modern financial imagery. The user should feel that Cote Juros is calm, clear, and trustworthy.

Prefer:

- real people in everyday financial moments
- clean home office, desk, or neutral workplace settings
- natural expressions, not exaggerated celebration
- neutral clothes and professional environments
- subtle financial context, such as phone, notebook, card, contract, or laptop
- light backgrounds with controlled contrast
- blue, green, white, gray, and soft neutral accents

Avoid:

- call center imagery
- people holding piles of cash
- miracle approval visuals
- aggressive sales gestures
- fake handshake photos
- gold coins, safes, money rain, or lottery-like scenes
- heavy purple, neon, or crypto-style gradients
- anything that suggests Cote Juros is the lender

## Placeholder Map

`hero-credit-comparison-placeholder`

Use in the home hero. Look for a calm person comparing credit options on a phone or laptop, with a clean financial workspace.

`profile-section-placeholder`

Use on profile landing pages. Look for a person reviewing simple financial information, preferably in a neutral desk setting.

`trust-section-placeholder`

Use near compliance or trust blocks. Look for understated scenes involving documents, a phone, or a laptop, with no exaggerated legal or bank imagery.

`finance-ecosystem-placeholder`

Use only in Cote Finance or secondary ecosystem contexts. It should suggest organization and budgeting, not credit approval.

## Usage Rules

- Do not place Cote Finance imagery inside the main credit funnel.
- Do not use a bank-counter or lender-style hero on Cote Juros.
- Do not use imagery that implies approval, cash release, or instant money.
- Prefer one strong image per page over several generic decorative images.
- Keep image crops calm and spacious so copy remains the main conversion driver.
